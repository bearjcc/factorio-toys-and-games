# Art and attribution (planned)

v0.1.0 uses placeholder icons from Factorio `base` via `__base__/graphics/...` paths. Do not redistribute Wube DLC or Space Age art in this mod.

Future optional sources (not vendored yet):

- **Crushing Industry** crusher graphics (GNU LGPLv3) if we replace the placeholder `tg-crusher` sprite.
- **Bio Industries** farm/greenhouse art (MIT) for `tg-dye-greenhouse`.
- **Baketorio** plant-style icons (MIT) for paper mill / printer polish.

We do not plan to use Bob's Mods or Industrial Revolution 3 art (NC-ND).
